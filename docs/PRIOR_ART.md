## Located Frontier

The frozen Keri quaternary/quinary table records the `K_4(8,4)` cell as
`w 13-28 m`. Its bibliography mapping attributes:

- lower bound 13 to Keri, 2008; and
- upper bound 28 to Ostergard, 1991.

The parsed frozen table database has SHA-256
`7efb0122b7d6a60302194455953591c3e9842a8e7eff9b6f8f49858de3f7522c`.
The exact extracted record is preserved in
`data/audited-bound-record.json`.

## Current Refresh

Gijswijt and Polak's 2025-2026 semidefinite lower-bound work retains 13 for
this cell. The 2026 `coldcase` upper-bound paper explicitly treats alphabet
sizes 5 through 21, so it does not report a quaternary replacement.

Searches for the exact parameter and a 27-word construction located no
accessible primary-source equivalent. This negative search is bounded and
does not exclude inaccessible, unpublished, unindexed, or later work.
