\begin{abstract}
We give a quaternary length-8 covering code with 27 words and covering
radius 4. Three exhaustive verifier paths check all $4^8=65536$ ambient
words. Relative to the audited table interval
$13 \leq K_4(8,4) \leq 28$, the construction proves
$K_4(8,4) \leq 27$. A retained 26-word candidate has exact covering radius
5 and five radius-4 holes. No lower-bound improvement or exact value is
claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and prior frontier}

Let $K_q(n,R)$ be the least cardinality of a subset of
$\{0,\ldots,q-1\}^n$ whose radius-$R$ Hamming balls cover the full space.
For the quaternary cell considered here, one radius-4 ball contains
\[
  \sum_{i=0}^{4} \binom{8}{i}3^i = 7459
\]
of the $4^8=65536$ ambient words.

The frozen Keri table records the cell as \texttt{w 13-28 m}. Its
bibliography mapping attributes the lower bound 13 to Keri's 2008 work and
the upper bound 28 to Ostergard's 1991 construction work. A dated
2026 review found that the current semidefinite lower-bound work retains 13
for this cell, while the 2026 large-alphabet upper-bound campaign begins at
alphabet size 5 and does not cover the quaternary case.

\begin{theorem}
The displayed code has covering radius four. Consequently,
\[
  K_4(8,4) \leq 27.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The three verification programs included with the source package enumerate
all 65536 ambient words and compute each word's minimum Hamming distance to
the 27 centers. Each reports maximum minimum distance four and no uncovered
word at radius four.
\end{proof}

\section{Independent verification}

The first Python implementation represents words as integer tuples and
enumerates the ambient space with a Cartesian product. The second represents
words as strings and has separate parsing and distance loops. A C++ program
decodes every integer from 0 through 65535 in base four and computes direct
distances.

The two Python implementations agree on the complete nearest-distance
distribution:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrr}
d & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4\\
\hline
\# & 27 & 648 & 6750 & 33100 & 25011.
\end{array}
\]

\hline
\# & 26 & 624 & 6528 & 32533 & 25820 & 5.
\end{array}
\]
Its five radius-4 holes are
\[
\begin{split}
&02131221,\quad 12013220,\quad 13332010,\\
&20222020,\quad 33310232.
\end{split}
\]
This candidate is not a covering code. Five bounded repair runs ending at
the same hole count are construction-search records only and carry no
lower-bound implication.

\section{Reproducibility and limitations}

The source package contains the code fixtures, both Python verifiers, the
C++ verifier, machine-readable evidence, mutation tests, source attribution,
and a closed-world SHA-256 manifest. The complete local gate is
\texttt{make check}.

The result does not improve the lower bound 13, establish a 26-word code,
prove optimality of 27, or determine $K_4(8,4)$. The prior-art review cannot
exclude inaccessible, unpublished, unindexed, or later work. External
mathematical review and peer review remain pending.
